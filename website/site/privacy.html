Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version: TripperDash++ does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your location never leaves your device except as network requests you initiate to Apple and OpenStreetMap to make the map and routing work.

Who we are

TripperDash++ ("the app") is an independent iOS application that streams turn-by-turn navigation to a Royal Enfield Tripper TFT dash. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information the app uses

  • Location. The app uses your precise location to show your position on the map, follow your route, provide turn-by-turn and voice guidance, and keep navigation running while your screen is locked. Your location is processed on your device. It is used to request map tiles and routes (see below) but is never sent to any server operated by us, and it is never stored or shared for advertising or tracking.
  • Saved app data. Routes, favorites, recent destinations, your ride history (the track and totals of each ride from the last 30 days), your bike's Wi-Fi name, and your settings are stored locally on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Third-party services the app contacts

To function, the app makes network requests directly from your device to:

  • Apple Maps (MapKit) — for route calculation and place search. Handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — for map tiles. Map imagery is © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Open-Meteo — for weather along your route (no account, no personal identifiers).
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass — for speed cameras, average-speed sections and posted speed limits along your route.

These requests may include coordinates necessary to return the right map, route, or data for where you are. We do not operate these services and we do not receive a copy of these requests.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a backend server that receives your data.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Data retention & deletion

Because all app data lives only on your device, deleting the app removes it. Rides older than 30 days are deleted automatically, and you can delete any ride sooner in Ride history. You can also clear saved routes, favorites, and cached map tiles from within the app's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
