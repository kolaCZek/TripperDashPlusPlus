1.0.6

Next version

✨ New

  • 3D map. The dash map now tilts 55° towards the road ahead, so you see about twice as far, with a blue sky over the horizon (dark at night). Your position marker lies on the map with it. Zoom out and the map flattens, like in Google Maps. On by default; turn it off in Settings → Map → 3D map.
  • Compass. The bottom row of the dash can show a compass tape instead of the route progress bar: Settings → Map → Bottom row (Route progress, Compass or Off). With Route progress, free rides show the compass, since there is no route to measure.
  • Sunset pill. A small sunset glyph and the local sunset time on the dash map when you will arrive after dark. It looks at where you will be along the route when the sun sets, not at your start or your destination, so a route across half a continent still gets the right time. It only appears when dusk (the sun 6° below the horizon) falls before your arrival, so there is no warning on a Nordic white night. It follows the Weather alerts switch.
  • Spoken speed-limit warning. New setting Speak speed-limit warnings in Voice guidance (off by default). The app says “Speed limit 50” once when you go over the posted limit, by the same margin that lights the sign. It works in navigation and free ride, in all 8 voice languages, and a turn prompt always takes priority.

🧭 Navigation

  • Share from Kurviger. Share a Kurviger route (Share → Route, a kurv.gr link) to TripperDash++ and it plans the route with all its points in order, including the shaping points, so the legs follow the curvy roads you planned. Routing between the points still uses Apple Maps.
  • Share from Calimoto. Share a Calimoto route link to TripperDash++ and it imports the whole route into Saved routes and plans it, like an imported GPX track.
  • Open GPX files from other apps. A GPX file shared from another app (an export from Kurviger, Calimoto, Scenic, a file in Files or Mail) now offers TripperDash++ in the share sheet. It lands in Saved routes and is planned straight away, joining the route at the nearest point when you are already partway along. During a ride it is only saved.
  • Fuel adds a stop. While navigating, the Quick Ride widget’s Fuel button now adds the nearest fuel station as the next stop and keeps your destination and later stops, instead of replacing the route.
  • Speed limits on long routes. The posted limits are now fetched in 20 km pieces along the route, nearest first, and each piece shows up on the dash as soon as it arrives. One request for the whole route used to time out on long trips, so they got no limits at all. A piece that fails is retried on its own, and a reroute only fetches the new stretch. Thanks to Eduardo for the suggestion.
  • Fixed: with the phone mounted sideways or upside down, the map and the arrow on the dash turned 90° or 180° the wrong way when standing or riding slowly (where the map follows the compass). The compass now follows how the phone sits in its mount. Thanks to renansalenave for the report.

🌦️ Weather

  • Fixed: the weather pill (“Rain 10 km”) stayed on the dash after you stopped navigating and started a free ride, where “ahead” means nothing. It is now cleared on Stop, on arrival and when a free ride starts.

🔒 Dash link

  • Fixed: the app now ignores packets from other devices on the bike’s Wi-Fi (a basic check). Thanks to Eduardo for spotting it.

1.0.5

Latest TestFlight build

Shortcuts, a home-screen widget, a sturdier dash link and a way to support the app.

✨ New

  • Shortcuts and Siri. Actions to start a free ride, navigate to a favorite, a saved route or a place, stop the ride and read the ride status. A running navigation can be redirected in the background. An automation can start the free ride as soon as the phone joins the bike’s Wi-Fi.
  • Quick Ride widget. Home-screen buttons for Home, Work, Free Ride and Fuel (the nearest fuel station within 20 km).
  • Support the app. Settings → Support offers optional one-off tips. They unlock nothing; every feature stays free.
  • Turn arrow on the route line. The upcoming turn is drawn on the route in white, like in Google Maps, at the same size in town and at highway zoom.

🧭 Navigation

  • Fixed: after leaving the route, the reroute often led straight back the way you came. It now prefers a route that carries on in your direction of travel. If none exists, the dash says U-turn and you get a fresh try sooner.
  • Fixed: a stop removed from the dash menu was still drawn on the dash, and the route kept leading to it for several seconds. It is dropped at once and the route is recalculated from where you are.
  • Fixed: a route shared from Google Maps no longer shows a stale pin on its first stop, and waits briefly for a GPS fix so that a first stop right where you stand is dropped.
  • Fixed: the map preview of a saved route now updates when you edit its points.

🔌 Connection

  • Short Wi-Fi blips between the phone and the dash no longer end the session at once: the app waits a few seconds and drops the link only if it is really gone. The debug log now names the reason for every drop.
  • The look-ahead maneuver (the second turn on the dash) is now sent exactly like the real phone does, so the dash draws it reliably, and it is cleared when it no longer applies.

🧰 Diagnostics

  • Export log (Settings → Diagnostics) now works and is available in TestFlight builds, so testers can send a log of a problem ride. It is hidden in the App Store build.

1.0.4

TestFlight 17 Oct 2026 · App Store 2 Nov 2026

Ride history, editing the route mid-ride, a steadier dash picture and a sturdier bike connection.

🕘 Ride history

  • Every ride of the last 30 days is kept, with its map, distance, moving time and average speed. Save it as a route or export it as GPX.
  • Fixed: standing still with poor GPS (garage, under a roof) no longer adds distance.

🧭 Planning & navigation

  • Edit the route while navigating: add, remove or reorder stops without stopping the ride. The stream, ride stats and dash stay up.
  • Save a planned route straight from the planner, and add a stop to a saved route from its detail view.
  • Fixed: the dash and the Lock Screen Live Activity said “Free ride” during navigation; they now show your destination.

📺 Dash picture

  • The stream now runs at 4 fps with a keyframe every second, matching the stock app. The picture no longer freezes or breaks into fragments.
  • GPS runs in car-navigation mode while riding, so fewer fixes are missed and the map jumps less.
  • Overlay text has a solid backdrop, stays inside the dash’s round visible area and is no longer clipped.
  • Fixed: a blurry map after a manual zoom-out at highway speed, and “You’ve arrived” drawn upside down.

🔌 Connection

  • If iOS stays on another Wi-Fi, the app tells you to join the bike’s network in Settings.
  • A dash that stops answering while the link looks fine is detected and reconnected; a link that keeps dropping no longer retries forever; start, stop and reconnect races are closed.

🛡️ Ride safety

  • Stopping a ride needs a 1-second hold, so a gloved tap or a bump of the mount can’t end it. Cancelling an unsaved plan asks first.

✨ Settings

  • Rename a saved bike. Demo mode now previews the real round dash.

Earlier beta builds

These only ever existed in TestFlight, before the first App Store release.

1.0.3

Builds 1–7, 2 Sep – 28 Sep 2026. Replaced by 1.0.4; never on the App Store.

Stability: the map, the connection and the locked screen.

🗺️ Map & ride

  • Fixed: the map going blank for 10–30 seconds, a mid-ride crash, and a blank map on the “While Using” location permission.
  • The dash keeps the link with the screen locked on “While Using”, so “Always” is no longer needed.
  • Free ride now shows posted speed limits and speed cameras. Heavier work runs off the main thread.

📷 Alerts

  • Average-speed sections get a panel with your average, a progress bar and the distance left.
  • The weather pill adds Frost risk, Ice and Crosswind.

🔌 Connection

  • A fresh connect retries through the dash’s boot delay, reconnects wait for the Wi-Fi address, and “Dash not responding — try the ignition” replaces an endless “Reconnecting…”.
  • Joining the wrong Wi-Fi is detected straight away. About shows the build number.
