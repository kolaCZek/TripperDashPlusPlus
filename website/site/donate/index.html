Thank you for riding with TripperDash++.

Made in my spare time

I'm really glad you like the app. I'm a rider too, and I build TripperDash++ after work and at weekends because I wanted proper navigation on my own Tripper dash.

Every feature is free, with no ads, no account and no tracking. The costs are real, though: the Apple developer membership and many evenings of testing on the bike.

If the app makes your rides better and you'd like to give something back, a coffee on Ko-fi helps me keep it going and keep adding features. It's completely optional, and every bit is appreciated.
